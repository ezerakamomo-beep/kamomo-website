Admissions

Join Kasenyi SS

How entry works

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Visit the school, call the office, or send a message with the learner’s current class.

  2. 02

    Submit documents

    Bring a birth certificate, previous school reports, two passport photos and the filled form.

  3. 03

    Assessment

    O-Level and A-Level entry may include a placement interview or written exercise.

  4. 04

    Offer & reporting

    Successful applicants receive a joining letter, fee structure and reporting date.

What to bring

  • Completed application form (online or collected from the office).
  • Copy of birth certificate and most recent school reports.
  • Two recent passport photographs.
  • Leaving certificate from the previous school, where applicable.
  • UNEB result slip for Senior 5 applicants.

Fees are published each year by the Board of Governors. Please call +256 772 494 855 or visit the bursar’s office for the current structure.
